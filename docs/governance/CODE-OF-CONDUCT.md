# Code of Conduct

> Building a respectful, inclusive and professional learning community.

---

# Our Commitment

InfraDev Academy is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and collaborative environment for everyone.

We believe that great technology is built by great people working together with professionalism, curiosity and mutual respect.

Our community values constructive dialogue, continuous learning and responsible knowledge sharing.

---

# Our Values

Every interaction within the project should reflect the following principles.

## Respect

Treat everyone with courtesy and professionalism.

Different backgrounds, experience levels and opinions strengthen our community.

---

## Collaboration

Knowledge grows when shared.

Help others learn.

Ask questions.

Share experiences.

Document discoveries.

---

## Curiosity

Technology evolves constantly.

We encourage experimentation, research and lifelong learning.

There are no "stupid questions".

There are opportunities to learn.

---

## Professionalism

Communicate clearly.

Respect deadlines when applicable.

Provide constructive feedback.

Accept reviews respectfully.

Focus on improving ideas rather than criticizing people.

---

## Responsibility

Contributors are responsible for the accuracy of the information they publish.

When mistakes are identified, correcting them is part of the learning process.

---

# Expected Behavior

Examples include:

- Be respectful.
- Be patient.
- Encourage beginners.
- Welcome constructive discussions.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Cite reliable sources whenever appropriate.
- Document decisions.
- Preserve educational quality.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Personal attacks.
- Harassment.
- Discrimination.
- Hate speech.
- Offensive language.
- Intimidation.
- Deliberate misinformation.
- Publishing confidential information.
- Plagiarism.
- Any form of unethical behavior.

---

# Technical Discussions

Healthy technical disagreement is encouraged.

However:

Discuss ideas.

Do not attack people.

Support arguments with technical reasoning whenever possible.

---

# Real-World Case Studies

InfraDev Academy frequently uses real-world technical scenarios.

Contributors must:

- Remove confidential information.
- Anonymize organizations.
- Protect customer privacy.
- Respect intellectual property.
- Preserve educational intent.

---

# Educational Integrity

Our goal is not simply to provide answers.

We strive to explain:

- Why.
- How.
- When.
- Trade-offs.
- Best practices.

Learning is more valuable than memorization.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence may be used as a productivity tool.

However, contributors remain responsible for reviewing, validating and understanding any generated content before publication.

Accuracy always takes precedence over speed.

---

# Conflict Resolution

When disagreements occur:

1. Discuss respectfully.
2. Seek technical evidence.
3. Focus on the project's goals.
4. Request a review when necessary.

The objective is collaboration, not competition.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers may:

- Request revisions.
- Reject contributions.
- Remove inappropriate content.
- Suspend participation when necessary.

These actions aim to preserve a healthy learning environment.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub repositories
- Documentation
- Issues
- Discussions
- Pull Requests
- Educational content
- Community spaces
- Future communication channels

---

# Final Statement

InfraDev Academy exists to help people become better technology professionals.

Every contribution should make both the project and its community stronger.
