# Career

The Career domain contains educational and practical resources about professional development, learning strategy, portfolio building, technical positioning, employability and long-term growth in technology.

It connects technical capability with evidence, communication, experience and responsible career planning.

---

## Purpose

This collection exists to help learners understand how to transform technical learning into professional development.

The domain supports:

- career foundations;
- technical positioning;
- learning strategy;
- role exploration;
- portfolio development;
- curriculum vitae;
- LinkedIn and professional profiles;
- interview preparation;
- job-search organization;
- professional communication;
- internships;
- junior roles;
- career transitions;
- certification planning;
- technical growth;
- mentoring foundations;
- ethical positioning;
- continuous development.

Career content should connect ambition with evidence, preparation and realistic execution.

## Domain Principles

Career content should follow the principles below.

### 1. Evidence before Claim

Professional positioning should be supported by demonstrable work.

### 2. Clarity before Volume

A focused profile is usually stronger than an unfocused list of technologies.

### 3. Learning through Delivery

Study should produce artifacts, projects, notes, laboratories or case studies whenever practical.

### 4. Realistic Progression

Career plans should consider current level, time, responsibilities and available opportunities.

### 5. Consistency

Professional growth depends on sustained execution rather than isolated intensity.

### 6. Ethics

Professional materials should remain truthful.

### 7. Adaptability

Career plans may evolve as interests, opportunities and evidence change.

### 8. Learning by Engineering

Career development should connect:

```text
Goal

↓

Current Evidence

↓

Skill Gap

↓

Learning Plan

↓

Practical Delivery

↓

Professional Positioning

↓

Feedback and Improvement
```

## Responsible Use of Real Cases

Real professional cases may support portfolio evidence when:
