# Licensing Policy

> Protecting knowledge while encouraging learning and collaboration.

---

# Purpose

This document defines the licensing strategy adopted by InfraDev Academy.

Different project assets have different objectives and therefore may use different licenses.

The goal is to balance:

- Open collaboration
- Educational accessibility
- Intellectual property protection
- Commercial sustainability

---

# Licensing Philosophy

Knowledge should be shared whenever possible.

However, creating high-quality educational content requires continuous investment.

InfraDev Academy adopts a hybrid licensing model that supports both community growth and long-term sustainability.

---

# Asset Categories

The Academy is composed of different types of assets.

Each category may adopt its own licensing model.

---

# 1. Source Code

Examples:

Applications

Libraries

Automation

Scripts

Templates

Tools

Default License

MIT License

Reason

Encourages learning, reuse and community contributions while preserving copyright notices.

---

# 2. Technical Documentation

Examples:

Technical Notes (NT)

Guides

Architecture

References

Handbooks

Default License

Copyright © InfraDev Academy

All rights reserved unless otherwise stated.

Reason

Documentation is part of the educational product and represents a significant intellectual investment.

Specific documents may be published under more permissive licenses when strategically appropriate.

---

# 3. Laboratories

Examples:

LAB-0001

LAB-0002

LAB-0100

Default License

Copyright © InfraDev Academy

Educational use permitted unless otherwise specified.

---

# 4. Case Studies

Case Studies are based on real experiences.

Requirements:

- Client identities must be anonymized.
- Sensitive information must be removed.
- Proprietary information must never be disclosed.
- Educational value must be preserved.

Ownership of the original systems and products remains with their respective owners.

---

# 5. Books and E-books

Commercial products.

All rights reserved unless otherwise stated.

Redistribution without permission is prohibited.

---

# 6. Courses

Commercial educational products.

Protected by copyright.

Recording, redistribution or resale without authorization is prohibited.

---

# 7. Premium Content

Examples:

Advanced Guides

Exclusive Templates

Professional Documentation

Mentoring Materials

Reserved for licensed customers.

---

# 8. Website Content

Unless otherwise specified, website content follows the same licensing policy as the corresponding documentation.

---

# Third-Party Content

InfraDev Academy respects third-party intellectual property.

External assets should only be used when:

- licensed appropriately;
- public domain;
- original;
- properly attributed.

---

# Open Source Contributions

Community contributions remain under the project's applicable license.

Contributors retain copyright over their own work while granting the project the necessary rights to distribute it under the project's licensing model.

Contribution details are described in:

CONTRIBUTING.md

---

# Trademarks

InfraDev Academy name and visual identity are trademarks of the project.

Use of the name or logo must not imply endorsement without authorization.

---

# Commercial Use

Commercial use of open-source code is permitted when allowed by its license.

Commercial redistribution of proprietary documentation or premium content requires prior authorization.

---

# Educational Use

Educational institutions may reference public documentation while respecting copyright and attribution requirements.

---

# Future Licensing

As the Academy evolves, additional licensing models may be adopted for specific products or partnerships.

Any significant licensing changes must be documented through version updates.

---

# Questions

Licensing questions should be directed to the project maintainers.

Future contact channels will be published on the official website.

---

# Final Statement

InfraDev Academy believes that open knowledge and sustainable business are complementary.

A healthy balance between both ensures continuous investment in quality educational resources.
