## 1. Purpose

This Standard defines the editorial requirements adopted by the InfraDev Academy for the creation, review, publication and evolution of Official Publications.

Its purpose is to ensure that every publication provides a consistent, readable, traceable and maintainable experience.

---

## 2. Scope

This Standard applies directly to the Official Publications collection.

Examples include:

- Project Manifesto;
- Vision;
- Mission;
- Core Values;
- Brand Principles;
- future institutional publications.

Its principles may also be used as a baseline for other document families when appropriate.

This Standard does not define detailed grammar, Markdown formatting, file naming or complete document lifecycle rules. Those responsibilities belong to dedicated Standards.

---

## 3. Editorial Principles

All Official Publications shall follow the principles described below.

### 3.1 Consistency

Documents belonging to the same collection should share a recognizable structure, terminology and presentation.

### 3.2 Readability

Content should be organized so that readers can understand the publication without unnecessary effort.

### 3.3 Traceability

Every publication should clearly identify its edition, version, release and current status.

### 3.4 Maintainability

Documents should be structured so that future revisions can be performed without unnecessary rework.

### 3.5 Accessibility

Language and organization should make technical and institutional ideas understandable to the intended audience.

### 3.6 Longevity

Editorial decisions should support the long-term evolution of the publication and the ecosystem.

### 3.7 Institutional Coherence

Every publication should remain aligned with the identity, vision, mission and principles of the InfraDev Academy.
