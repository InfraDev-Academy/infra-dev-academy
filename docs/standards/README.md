# Standards

The Standards collection defines the official conventions, requirements and repeatable practices adopted by the InfraDev Academy.

Standards describe how work must be performed to preserve consistency, quality, traceability and maintainability across the ecosystem.

---

## Purpose

This collection exists to transform architectural and institutional principles into practical rules that can be consistently applied.

Standards support:

- documentation production;
- editorial consistency;
- repository organization;
- naming conventions;
- document maintenance;
- contributor onboarding;
- long-term governance.

---

## Scope

The Standards collection governs the creation and maintenance of institutional, technical and educational documents produced by the InfraDev Academy.

Each Standard addresses a specific responsibility and should avoid overlapping with other Standards.

## Document Responsibilities

Different document families serve different purposes within the InfraDev Academy.

### Architecture Decision Records

ADRs explain why significant architectural, governance, platform or editorial decisions were made.

### Standards

Standards define the official rules and requirements that must be followed.

### Guides

Guides explain how people can apply Standards and interact with the ecosystem.

### Official Publications

Official Publications communicate the institutional identity, vision and principles of the Academy.

---

## Navigation

Contributors creating or reviewing Official Publications should begin with:

1. STD-001 — Editorial Standard
2. GUIDE-002 — Editorial Handbook
3. STD-002 — Writing Standard, when available
4. STD-003 — Markdown Standard, when available

The Editorial Standard defines the required structure.

The Editorial Handbook explains how to apply it in practice.

---

## Governance

An Active Standard is considered an official requirement of the InfraDev Academy.

Changes that materially alter a Standard must be reviewed and documented.

Minor corrections may be recorded through the project Decision Log.

Changes that affect the architecture or responsibilities of an entire document family may require an Architecture Decision Record.
