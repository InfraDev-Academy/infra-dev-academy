# Academy

The Academy collection contains the living institutional documents of the InfraDev Academy.

These documents represent the current institutional identity, direction and internal reference of the organization.

Unlike Official Publications, which preserve published editions, the Academy documents reflect the current active institutional state.

---

## Purpose

This collection exists to maintain the living institutional foundation of the InfraDev Academy.

It provides current references for:

- Vision;
- Mission;
- Core Values;
- Brand Principles;
- institutional identity;
- educational philosophy;
- strategic direction;
- future organizational principles.

The collection supports contributors, maintainers, editors and project leaders when evaluating whether new work remains aligned with the Academy.

---

## Scope

The Academy collection contains institutional documents that describe the current state of the organization.

Examples include:

- `VISION.md`;
- `MISSION.md`;
- `CORE-VALUES.md`;
- `BRAND-PRINCIPLES.md`;
- future institutional reference documents.

These files may evolve as the Academy matures.

They should remain aligned with the corresponding Official Publications.

---

## Living Institutional Documents

Documents in this collection are considered living institutional references.

They represent the most current approved version of the Academy's:

- purpose;
- direction;
- values;
- principles;
- strategic identity.

These documents may be updated without creating a completely new institutional publication every time a minor improvement is required.

However, meaningful institutional changes must remain traceable.

---

## Relationship with Official Publications

The Academy collection and the Official Publications collection serve different purposes.

### Academy Documents

Represent the current living institutional state.

They answer:

> What does the InfraDev Academy currently believe, pursue and practice?

### Official Publications

Preserve formally published institutional editions.

They answer:

> How was this institutional position officially published and how has it evolved through editions?

Example:

```text
docs/academy/VISION.md

Current living institutional Vision

↓

docs/publications/OP-002-Vision.md

Official published edition of the Vision
```

The living document may evolve operationally.

The Official Publication preserves formal edition history through:

- publication metadata;
- Edition Notes;
- project releases;
- Git history.

## Institutional Alignment

New documents, features, learning resources and structural changes should be evaluated against the Academy's institutional references.

Questions may include:

- Does this support the Vision?
- Does this contribute to the Mission?
- Is it compatible with the Core Values?
- Does it respect the Brand Principles?
- Does it improve the experience of future readers, contributors or maintainers?
- Can the Academy maintain it responsibly?

Institutional alignment does not require every activity to relate directly to every document.

It requires the ecosystem to evolve without contradicting its foundational direction.

---

## Updating Institutional Documents

Before updating a living institutional document:

1. identify the reason for the change;
2. verify whether the change is editorial or institutional;
3. review related Official Publications;
4. evaluate the impact on other collections;
5. record the decision;
6. complete the required review;
7. update related references when necessary.

Minor wording improvements may be recorded through the Decision Log or Git history.

Meaningful changes to institutional direction may require:

- a new Official Publication edition;
- updated Edition Notes;
- Product Review;
- Institutional Review;
- an Architecture Decision Record when structural consequences exist.

---

## Consistency Rules

Living institutional documents should:

- use consistent terminology;
- remain compatible with Official Publications;
- avoid duplicating operational procedures;
- avoid replacing Governance documents;
- avoid defining technical Standards;
- preserve institutional clarity;
- maintain traceable revisions.

The Academy collection defines institutional direction.

It does not replace the responsibilities of other collections.

## Maintenance

This collection should be reviewed when:

- institutional direction changes;
- the Academy enters a new maturity stage;
- new strategic commitments are approved;
- terminology becomes inconsistent;
- Official Publications receive new editions;
- Governance or architecture changes affect institutional responsibilities.

The collection should remain concise enough to guide decisions and complete enough to preserve institutional coherence.
