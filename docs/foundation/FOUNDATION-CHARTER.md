# Foundation Charter

> The Constitution of InfraDev Academy

---

# 1. Purpose

The Foundation Charter defines the principles, values, objectives and long-term direction of the InfraDev Academy.

Every technical, educational and business decision made within the project should remain consistent with this document.

This Charter represents the highest-level reference for the Academy and serves as the foundation upon which all future documentation, software, educational materials and products will be built.

---

# 2. Why InfraDev Academy Exists

Technology evolves rapidly.

Programming languages change.

Frameworks become obsolete.

Infrastructure is continuously transformed.

However, one element remains valuable over time:

**Well-structured knowledge.**

InfraDev Academy exists to transform practical experience into organized, reusable and continuously evolving technical knowledge.

Rather than simply teaching tools, the Academy aims to develop professionals capable of understanding concepts, solving problems and making informed engineering decisions.

---

# 3. Mission

To transform practical experience into structured technical knowledge that is accessible, reproducible and valuable for professionals at every stage of their careers.

---

# 4. Vision

To become an internationally recognized technical learning platform where theory and practice coexist through documentation, laboratories, projects and real-world case studies.

---

# 5. Long-Term Vision

InfraDev Academy is not intended to become only:

- a documentation website;
- an online course platform;
- a YouTube channel;
- a book collection.

Instead, it aims to become a complete knowledge ecosystem composed of:

- Documentation
- Books
- Courses
- Laboratories
- Software
- Templates
- Automation
- Open-source tools
- Professional community
- Premium educational products

---

# 6. Core Values

## Learn Continuously

Learning never ends.

Every solved problem becomes an opportunity to improve the knowledge base.

---

## Practice Before Memorization

Understanding is achieved through execution.

Every theoretical concept should eventually be practiced.

---

## Build Real Solutions

Knowledge should produce tangible results.

Students should leave the Academy having built projects, not only completed lessons.

---

## Share Knowledge Responsibly

Knowledge grows when documented and shared responsibly.

Whenever possible, educational content should be open and reusable.

---

## Think Like an Engineer

Technology decisions should always prioritize:

- simplicity
- maintainability
- documentation
- reproducibility
- long-term sustainability

---

# 7. Educational Philosophy

InfraDev Academy adopts the LPBT model.

```
Learn
↓

Practice
↓

Build
↓

Teach
```

Learning becomes complete only after the student is capable of explaining the concept to someone else.

# 10. Documentation Philosophy

Documentation is considered a first-class deliverable.

Code without documentation is incomplete.

Documentation should:

- be versioned;
- be reviewed;
- be reproducible;
- evolve together with the project.

---

# 11. Quality Standards

Every document should:

✔ have a clear objective;

✔ define its audience;

✔ provide references;

✔ include practical examples whenever possible;

✔ maintain editorial consistency;

✔ remain technically accurate.

---

# 12. Internationalization

InfraDev Academy is designed from its foundation to support multiple languages.

Primary languages:

- Portuguese (Brazil)
- English

Future translations should preserve technical consistency across all editions.

---

# 13. Sustainability

The Academy adopts a hybrid sustainability model.

Open educational resources will coexist with commercial products, ensuring continuous investment in the evolution of the platform.

Possible revenue streams include:

- Premium courses
- Digital books
- Templates
- Professional documentation
- Consulting
- Community memberships

---

# 14. Community

The project encourages collaboration based on respect, professionalism and knowledge sharing.

Every contributor is expected to uphold the Academy's values.

---

# 15. Decision Making

Important technical and organizational decisions must be documented through Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

The goal is to preserve the reasoning behind each significant choice.

---

# 16. Evolution

This Charter is a living document.

It should evolve together with the Academy while preserving its original principles.

Major changes require a documented review and version update.

---

# Final Statement

InfraDev Academy exists to demonstrate that practical experience, when transformed into structured documentation, can become one of the most valuable assets for learning, teaching and building technology.
