# Project Glossary

> Common terminology used across InfraDev Academy.

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the official terminology adopted throughout the InfraDev Academy ecosystem.

Its objectives are:

- standardize technical language;
- avoid ambiguity;
- facilitate collaboration;
- maintain consistency across documentation.

Whenever a new recurring term is introduced, it should be added to this document.

---

# Documentation

## ADR

Architecture Decision Record

Documents that register important architectural and organizational decisions.

---

## PRD

Product Requirements Document

Defines product requirements, goals, users and success criteria.

---

## NT

Technical Note

A document focused on explaining a concept, technology or technical topic.

---

## LAB

Laboratory

A practical, reproducible exercise designed to reinforce learning.

---

## CASE

Case Study

A document describing a real-world scenario, its context, diagnosis, solution and lessons learned.

---

## RFC

Request for Comments

A proposal document used to discuss significant changes before implementation.

---

## KB

Knowledge Base

The complete collection of technical knowledge maintained by InfraDev Academy.

---

## Handbook

A collection of standards, guides and internal documentation.

---

# Product

## MVP

Minimum Viable Product

The smallest version of a product capable of delivering value.

---

## Feature

A functional capability delivered by the product.

---

## Release

A published version of the project.

---

## Milestone

A significant development objective composed of one or more releases.

---

## Backlog

A prioritized list of ideas, improvements and future work.

---

## Sprint

A defined period dedicated to achieving a specific goal.

---

# Development

## Commit

A recorded change in the Git history.

---

## Branch

An isolated line of development.

---

## Merge

The integration of one branch into another.

---

## Pull Request (PR)

A proposal to merge changes after review.

---

## Repository

A version-controlled project managed through Git.

---

## Semantic Versioning

Versioning strategy using:

MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH

Example:

1.4.2

---

# Infrastructure

## Container

An isolated execution environment.

---

## Virtual Machine (VM)

A software-defined computer running on physical hardware.

---

## Infrastructure

The hardware and software resources supporting applications.

---

## Environment

A configured execution context.

Examples:

Development

Testing

Homologation

Production

---

# Educational

## Learning Path

A structured sequence of learning content.

---

## Module

A group of related topics.

---

## Lesson

An individual educational unit.

---

## Exercise

An activity intended to reinforce knowledge.

---

## Assessment

A method used to evaluate learning progress.

---

# Quality

## Review

The process of validating technical and editorial quality.

---

## Validation

Confirmation that expected results have been achieved.

---

## Reproducibility

The ability to repeat an experiment or laboratory with the same expected outcome.

---

# Business

## Open Source

Software distributed under an open license.

---

## Premium Content

Educational content available through commercial licensing.

---

## Community

People who contribute to or benefit from the Academy.

---

## Ecosystem

The complete collection of products, documentation, services and community surrounding InfraDev Academy.
